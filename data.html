data

Data refers to raw, unprocessed facts, figures, symbols, or observations collected for analysis, reasoning, or reference. It is essentially a collection of "givens" (the plural of the Latin word datum), such as numbers, words, measurements, or descriptions that, in their raw state, may not carry specific meaning. These facts can be gathered from various sources, including surveys, sensors, or, in computing, stored electronically in databases or files. When data is organized, processed, and interpreted, it transforms into information, providing valuable insights for decision-making. Common examples of data include sales figures, customer names, temperature readings, test scores, and raw video or audio files.
